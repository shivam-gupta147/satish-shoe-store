Who we are

Run by the Gupta family, since day one

Satish Shoe Store sits right next to Kangra Mandir in the Main Bazaar — a shop most people in town could point you to without thinking twice. Ajay Gupta and his elder son Shubham Gupta run it together, and between them they've put together a wide, genuinely useful range of footwear rather than a token shelf of each brand.

The idea is a simple one: good shoes, honestly priced, with someone behind the counter who actually knows the stock. Come in looking for school shoes, wedding formals or a pair for the morning walk, and they'll point you straight to the right shelf.

Ajay Gupta & Shubham Gupta Owners, Satish Shoe Store

What's where

Three floors, sorted by who's shopping

The shop is split by floor rather than by brand, so families can head straight to the right section instead of hunting through everything.

Ground Floor

Kids & Ladies Footwear

The ground floor covers comfortable, hard-wearing shoes for kids and toddlers alongside a full range of women's footwear, from everyday slippers to occasion wear.

  • School shoes
  • Sandals & floaters
  • First walkers
  • Sports shoes for kids
  • Ladies slippers
  • Punjabi jootis
  • Heels
  • Ladies sandals
First Floor

Men's footwear

Upstairs covers every occasion a man might need shoes for — from the office to a wedding to a Sunday hike — casual, formal and sports, all under one roof.

  • Casual shoes
  • Formal & office wear
  • Sports & running shoes
  • Sandals & slippers
Second Floor

Extra stock

The top floor holds additional inventory that isn't out on display — extra sizes, older stock and slower-moving lines. Ask at the counter and staff will check upstairs for you.

  • Extra stock
  • Rare sizes
  • Seasonal storage
